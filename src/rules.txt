Texas Hold'em Poker: Official Rules & Logic

1. Overview
Texas Hold'em is a community card game. Each player is dealt two private cards (Hole Cards), and five community cards are dealt face-up on the board. The goal is to make the best five-card hand possible using any combination of the seven available cards.

2. Game Setup
The Dealer Button: A disk (the "button") identifies which player is the nominal dealer. It rotates clockwise after every hand.
The Blinds: Before cards are dealt, two forced bets are posted:
Small Blind (SB): Posted by the player to the left of the button.
Big Blind (BB): Posted by the player to the left of the Small Blind (usually double the SB).

3. The Rounds of Play
Phase 1: Pre-Flop
Each player receives two cards face down. Betting starts with the player to the left of the Big Blind ("Under the Gun").

Actions: Players can Fold, Call (match the BB), or Raise.
Phase 2: The Flop
Three community cards are dealt face-up in the center.

Actions: Betting starts with the first active player to the left of the button. Players can Check (if no bet is made) or Bet.
Phase 3: The Turn
A fourth community card is dealt face-up.

Actions: Another round of betting ensues, similar to the Flop.
Phase 4: The River
The fifth and final community card is dealt face-up.

Actions: The final round of betting takes place.
Phase 5: The Showdown
If more than one player remains after the final bet, they reveal their cards. The player with the highest-ranking five-card hand wins the pot. If hands are identical, the pot is split.

4. Player Actions
Check: Pass the action to the next player without betting (only possible if no one else has bet).
Bet: Place the first wager of the current round.
Call: Match the current bet amount.
Raise: Increase the size of the current bet.
Fold: Discard the hand and forfeit any interest in the pot.

5. Logic for Developers (Project Implementation)
If you are coding this game, keep these logic rules in mind:

The 5-Card Rule: A hand is always exactly 5 cards. If two players have the same "Three of a Kind," the remaining two cards (Kickers) determine the winner.
Ace Logic: An Ace can be the highest card (A-K-Q-J-10 straight) or the lowest card (5-4-3-2-A straight, known as a "Wheel").
Table Stakes: A player cannot be forced to fold because they don't have enough chips to call a bet. They can go All-In, creating a Side Pot they are not eligible to win.
